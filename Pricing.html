Payment status

Read before paying

  • Do not assume a payment is complete until the website shows a verified confirmation.
  • A pending request or screenshot is not proof that funds were received.
  • Never share your password, OTP, UPI PIN, card PIN or banking credentials with support staff.

Online payment availability depends on the currently deployed payment integration. This policy page does not itself enable payments or wallet credits.

Privacy & uploaded files

Only upload documents and photos that are necessary for the tool you are using. Avoid uploading another person's information unless you have their permission.

  • Before using a tool, review its page for any notice about file processing or external services.
  • Do not assume that every tool processes files locally or that files are never transmitted. Processing may differ by tool and by any connected third-party service.
  • Do not enter passwords, OTPs, bank PINs or other secrets into forms.
  • If you believe your personal information was exposed or handled incorrectly, stop using the affected feature and contact the service center.

This is a general notice, not a claim that every tool has been independently audited. Specific storage, deletion and retention details should be published when verified for each service.

Accuracy & service limitations

  • Check all names, numbers, dates and document details before downloading or printing.
  • Generated templates and drafts may need manual review and may not be accepted by every organization.
  • AI-labelled or online-dependent features may require external services and can have limitations or outages.
  • Rates, calculations, templates and informational content are provided as tools, not as legal, financial or government advice.
  • Keep your own copy of important files. Do not rely on the website as the only storage location.

Refunds, failed payments & help

If you believe you were charged incorrectly, a payment was duplicated, or a service did not complete, keep the transaction reference and contact the service center for review. Refund eligibility and processing time depend on the verified transaction and applicable payment provider rules.

Do not send full card details, UPI PINs, OTPs or account passwords when asking for help.
